Privacy policy

Mandate: Nation Simulator · Effective 16 November 2026

Mandate is a strategy game that runs on your device. We, the developer, do not collect information about you: there are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no crash reporting, and your games stay on your device. The Android and iOS apps show ads provided by Google, and Google collects some device information to do that, as explained below. This policy explains what that means for your information.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, receive, sell or share personal information about you.

Advertising (Android and iOS apps)

The apps show ads from Google AdMob (Google LLC, and Google Ireland Limited in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland). Some ads are optional: you can choose to watch one for an in-game reward. Others appear full-screen at natural breaks, such as after loading a game, at most once every few minutes. The web and downloadable HTML versions of the game show no ads.

To show ads, measure them, limit how often you see the same ad and prevent fraud, Google's software in the app collects and processes:

  • device identifiers: your device's advertising ID (on Android) and an app-specific ID;
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location (city or region, not your precise location);
  • device and app information such as the device model, operating system, language and app version;
  • ad interactions: which ads were shown, viewed or tapped, and diagnostic data about ad performance.

Google may use this information to personalise ads where the law and your choices allow. Google processes it under the Google Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services. We do not receive this information. We only see totals, such as how many ads were shown and what they earned.

Your choices.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in Menu → Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • The iOS app does not ask for permission to track you, so it does not use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • Some US state privacy laws treat showing personalised ads as "sharing" personal information for targeted advertising. You can opt out with the device settings above.

What is stored on your device

Your saved games, settings and preferences, the country names you choose, and the questions you type to the in-game advisor are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. They never leave your device through the game. Uninstalling the app deletes them, and you can delete individual saves from Menu → Save / Load. Your ad consent choice is also stored on your device.

If your device's own backup service is switched on (for example Android backup to your Google account), the operating system may include this app's saved games in your personal backup. That backup is handled by your device's provider under its privacy policy; we never receive it.

Permissions

The app asks for no special permissions. On Android it uses the standard internet and network-state permissions, for ads, and the advertising ID permission that Google's ad software requires. It never asks for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and is not intended for them. Ads are limited to content rated suitable for teens and older.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and on this page with a new effective date.
